EpiOps™ Workshop:Building Automated Ingestion Pipelines

Replace manual downloads and copy-paste with reliable, scheduled ingestion of surveillance data feeds.

  • Landing lab, case, and syndromic feeds (CSV, HL7 v2, FHIR) into a raw layer
  • Validation checks, logging, and safe re-runs
  • Scheduling and alerting when a feed is late or malformed
Format: Live, hands-on · Half-day (4 hours)Delivery: Virtual or on-site

How EpiOps™ Training works

Built for practitioners, taught by a practitioner

Every session is designed around the realities of public health work: limited staff, legacy systems, and data that has to be right.

Learn the pattern

Short, focused instruction on the concept and why it matters for surveillance and reporting.

Build it hands-on

Guided labs using realistic, synthetic public health data, with no real patient information required.

Take it back to work

Leave with reusable templates, checklists, and a plan for applying it in your own agency.

About the instructor

Sai Kurmana

MPH, MS, CPHIMS · Founder & Lead Instructor, EpiOps™ Workshops & Training

Sai Kurmana, MPH, MS, CPHIMS, is a healthcare data executive and former Director of Health Surveillance and Research at a state health department, where he led data modernization initiatives supporting $31.5M in federal chronic disease programs. He created EpiOps™ Workshops & Training to teach health department teams how to build modern, sustainable public health data infrastructure.
